================================================================================
NOVEL CONTRIBUTION
================================================================================

Learnable hierarchical reconciliation matrices that replace traditional 
bottom-up/top-down aggregation with differentiable neural projections, 
ensuring probabilistic coherence across 4 hierarchy levels through a 
custom coherence-aware loss function.

Key Innovation:
- Neural projection networks learn optimal aggregation weights
- Joint optimization of forecasting accuracy and hierarchical consistency
- Differentiable reconciliation enables end-to-end training
- Probabilistic coherence across quantile predictions

================================================================================
CUSTOM COMPONENTS (in src/models/components.py)
================================================================================

1. TemporalAttention
   - Multi-head temporal attention mechanism
   - Adaptive weighting of historical patterns

2. HierarchicalReconciliationLayer (NOVEL)
   - Learnable projection networks for each hierarchy level
   - Coherence enforcement through neural architecture
   - Replaces traditional fixed aggregation rules

3. CoherenceLoss (NOVEL)
   - Custom loss function for hierarchical consistency
   - Penalizes variance across hierarchy levels

4. QuantileLoss
   - Pinball loss for probabilistic forecasting
   - Multiple quantile predictions

5. CombinedLoss
   - Joint optimization: α × Quantile Loss + β × Coherence Loss
   - Configurable weights (default: 0.7, 0.3)
